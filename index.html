Who I am

independent · AU · remote

I'm a self-directed engineer who learns by shipping real systems — not tutorials, but bots that touch mainnet, exploits that run, and games people can play.

Most of my work lives at the intersection of money and code: automated trading infrastructure, and the adversarial flip-side of it — finding the flaws that let value leak out of a protocol. I treat both the same way: build the smallest thing that proves the idea, measure it honestly, and keep only what survives contact with reality.

I'd rather tell you a strategy didn't work than dress up a backtest. That discipline is the reason the security work pays.
